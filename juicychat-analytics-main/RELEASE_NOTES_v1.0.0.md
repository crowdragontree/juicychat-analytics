# JuicyChat Analytics Tool v1.0.0

## New
- **A standalone analytics app.** Juicy Blueprint Studio 1.6.0's analytics, on its own: **Dashboard**, **Your Performance**, **JuicyChat Analysis**, **Engine Library** and **Settings**. No image analytics, no image site and no bot building.
- **One sign-in.** Paste a magic link in **Settings** and press **Sign in**. JuicyChat Analysis and Your Performance both use it.
- **Your Performance** reads one account: the one you signed in with.
- **Settings:** your JuicyChat sign-in, the automatic daily pulls with their times and time zone, JuicyChat Collection, and **Blocked tags**: bots with a blocked tag are not pulled, as in Blueprint Studio.
- **The JuicyChat scanner** installs itself once, from Settings (**Install scanner**), the first time you use the app. App updates keep it.
- **In the JuicyChat Creator Tool:** if you also have the Creator Tool (v1.5.1 or later), it shows an **Analytics** tab with this app in it. It keeps its own sign-in and its own data, and the two apps never pull from JuicyChat at the same time: one waits until the other is done.

## Not included
- Your Performance has no **App score** column and no **Prediction accuracy** card: both came from Blueprint Studio's bot-building scores.
- When to release has no **Release suggestions by series**: it changed the Weekly Blueprint.

## Compatibility
- Windows 64-bit and Linux 64-bit

## Updating
**Check for updates → Update & Restart** installs new versions.
